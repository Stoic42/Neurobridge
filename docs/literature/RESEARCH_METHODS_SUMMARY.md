### 4. 论文发表标准 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

**位置**: `LITERATURE_APPENDED_RESEARCH.md` 第1142-1248行

**核心内容**:

#### 相关会议
- **CHI (ACM SIGCHI)**
  - Acceptance rate: ~25%
  - Full papers: 8-10 pages
  - Review criteria: Novelty (30%), Methods (30%), Impact (20%), Clarity (20%)

- **ASSETS (ACM SIGACCESS)**
  - Acceptance rate: ~30-40%
  - Full papers: 10 pages, Short papers: 4 pages
  - Focus: Assistive technologies, accessibility

- **其他会议**
  - CSCW (Computer-Supported Cooperative Work)
  - ICMI (Multimodal Interaction)

#### 相关期刊
- **Journal of Autism and Developmental Disorders**
  - Impact factor: ~5
  - Format: IMRaD, 5,000-8,000 words
  - Requires RCT-level evidence

- **ACM Transactions on Computer-Human Interaction (TOCHI)**
  - Double-blind review
  - Focus on user studies

#### 论文格式
- **标准结构**:
  1. Abstract (150-250 words)
  2. Introduction
  3. Related Work (20-30 sources)
  4. Methods (Participants, Tools, Ethics)
  5. Results (Quantitative + Qualitative)
  6. Discussion
  7. References (APA or ACM style)

- **格式要求**:
  - ACM Template (LaTeX preferred)
  - 8-12 pages excluding references
  - Figures with alt text for accessibility
